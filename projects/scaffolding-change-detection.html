Scaffolding change-detection pipeline

Locked benchmark 0.88 Precision, recall, and F1 on 434 components
Field check 7 of 8 Real structural changes recovered
IP Inventor Listed on the pending patent; no filing number here

Problem

Industrial scaffolding changes between site visits. Catching what was added or removed by walking the structure, or by comparing 3D scans by hand, is slow and easy to miss. The intern work was to make that comparison systematic enough to replace those walkthroughs.

Approach

I built an end-to-end synthetic data pipeline for scaffolding inspection: it generates labeled scenes, simulates sensor scans, and detects structural change. Related work is patent-pending; I am listed as an inventor. Source, architecture internals, and patent claims stay off this site.

The problem was not scoped from a desk. I visited one of India’s top ten refineries twice to study how scaffolds are erected and to coordinate with an outside vendor running 3D scans on site. Those visits set the requirements for the change-detection system.

Result

On a locked benchmark of 434 components, the pipeline reached 0.88 precision, 0.88 recall, and 0.88 F1, and correctly recovered 7 of 8 real structural changes.

I then piloted the system on-site at the refinery, moving it toward production so engineers get faster, earlier visibility into structural change instead of relying on manual inspection walkthroughs.
