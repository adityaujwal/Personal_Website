Scaffolding change-detection pipeline

0.88 Precision, recall, and F1
Patent-pending No filing number or claims on this site

Problem

Industrial scaffolding changes between site visits. Catching what was added or removed by comparing 3D scans by hand is slow and easy to miss. The intern work was to make that comparison systematic.

Approach

I designed a computer vision and LiDAR pipeline that takes time-separated point-cloud scans of the same site, registers them, and flags structural elements that appeared or disappeared. I proposed and scoped the approach independently.

This page stays at that conceptual level. Source code, proprietary architecture, client names, and patent claims are not published here.

Result

The pipeline reached 0.88 precision, 0.88 recall, and 0.88 F1 on the change-detection task. Related work is patent-pending.
