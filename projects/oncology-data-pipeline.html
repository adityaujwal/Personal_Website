Oncology data pipeline

10M+ cells Scale of the oncology dataset
6 tables Exact-match joins

Problem

Oncology analysis only works if records from different tables actually refer to the same cells and patients. The raw data was large, high-dimensional, and split across sources that did not join cleanly on their own.

Approach

I built pipelines to clean, join, and transform biomedical tables so downstream modeling sat on a consistent dataset. That included exact-match logic across six tables covering more than 10 million cells, plus checks with statisticians and domain experts so the joins matched clinical assumptions — not just key equality.

Result

Exact match across six tables at 10M+ cells. The cleaned dataset supported analysis of treatment outcomes and survivability rather than stalling in wrangling.
