WendWay · Co-founder & COO · Jun 2024 – Oct 2025

WendWay

Path Research → product Peer-reviewed compliance work shipped as software
Field University pilots Iterated with instructors and students

Problem

Universities adopting AI needed a practical way to handle writing integrity. Research notes were not enough; schools needed something instructors and students could actually run.

Approach

The product started as a research project I led at the JMHC Center for Applied AI & Philanthropy (AI research intern, part-time, February 2024 – present). That work is under peer review. I co-founded WendWay to take the findings and ship a writing-integrity product for higher education.

I served as COO and as the platform’s main backend engineer. In a small early-stage team I also handled hiring, fundraising conversations, partnerships, and day-to-day go-to-market calls. We piloted with real universities and kept iterating from instructor and student feedback.

Result

A deployed product, piloted with universities — compliance research that left the lab. I was with the company from June 2024 through October 2025.
