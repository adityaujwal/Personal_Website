WendWay

Research → product Compliance tooling shipped, not just a paper
University pilots Iterated with academic stakeholders

Problem

Universities adopting AI needed a practical way to check policies and compliance workflows. Research notes were not enough; schools needed something they could actually run.

Approach

I co-founded WendWay as an early-stage company around ethical-AI compliance for education. On the engineering side I built a FastAPI backend for policy validation and compliance workflows. On the operations side I ran the product through pilot evaluations with academic stakeholders and changed the system from their feedback.

Result

A deployed product, piloted with universities — compliance research that left the research stage.
